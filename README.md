# 30 AI Money Prompts

**30 copy-paste prompts for Claude, ChatGPT, or Gemini — every one tied to a money outcome.** Land clients, close deals, get paid faster, automate admin, grow.

No email wall. No newsletter. MIT licensed. Just take them.

## What's inside

[`PROMPTS.md`](PROMPTS.md) — all 30 prompts, organized by outcome:

| Section | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Land Clients** | Cold outreach, LinkedIn positioning, case-study teasers, lead-gen |
| **Close & Price** | Proposals, objection handling, pricing conversations |
| **Get Paid Faster** | Invoice follow-ups, late-payment nudges that keep the relationship |

| **Grow** | Content, referrals, upsells |

## How to use

1. Open [`PROMPTS.md`](PROMPTS.md), pick a prompt for the job in front of you.
2. Swap every `[BRACKET]` with your real details — the specifics are what make it work.
3. Paste into Claude, ChatGPT, or Gemini and go.

Start with prompt **#7** (the late-invoice nudge). It's the one people say pays for the whole pack — which is a strange thing to say about a free pack, but you get the idea.
